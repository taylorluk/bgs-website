## What the leaders do that you now do too

**Pricing transparency.** SEO Sherpa publishes Google Ads budget ranges (AED 3,000–50,000+/mo)
and states management fees as 10–20% of spend. Digital Gravity answers 2026 pricing in its
FAQ. Every one of your 20 service pages now answers cost directly, using the $5,000/month
figure you already state publicly — and differentiates on charging a **fixed fee rather than
a percentage of spend**, which is a defensible competitive position against the percentage model.

**FAQ depth.** SEO Sherpa runs 13 questions per service page. You were on 4; now 8. This is
also the cheapest structured-data win available — all 20 service pages carry `FAQPage` markup,
so the new questions are eligible for rich results immediately.

**Buyer questions nobody else answers.** Account and data ownership, and commitment terms,
are the two questions every serious buyer asks and that **none** of the competitors reviewed
answer on-page. You now answer both on all 20 service pages. That is a genuine differentiator,
not a parity play.

## What the leaders still do that you don't

These need assets, not copy — I cannot create them:

1. **Platform certifications.** Google Partner, Meta Business Partner, HubSpot, TikTok. Digital
   Gravity displays six. These are applied for, and they are the single clearest trust gap.
2. **Named people.** Both leaders name their teams. All 40 of your posts are attributed to
   "BGS Marketing Team". The `Person` schema is already wired and waiting for real names.
3. **Review volume.** Digital Gravity shows 4.4 from 46 Google reviews. You show none.
4. **Awards and media.** MENA Search Awards run annually and are enterable.
5. **Verified client proof.** Your "50+ brands" and "300% ROAS" are unsourced and
   unverifiable. Digital Gravity's "500+ clients / 1,500+ projects" are equally unsourced but
   are backed by visible logos and review counts.
